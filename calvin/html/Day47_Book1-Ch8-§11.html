Day 47 — Book 1, Chapter 8, §11

0 — Orientation (1 minute) –

Yesterday ended on the strangest job title in theology: the librarians of the Christian Church — Christ's bitterest enemies, guarding Christ's book. And I left you a question overnight:

§1–2 made Scripture's MAJESTY the evidence. §11 makes the Gospels' LOWLY STYLE (simplicity) the evidence. How can lowness argue for credibility — and how is that paradox family to yesterday's (the enemy as custodian)?

Open today's section and collect your answer in the first three sentences. Calvin does not apologize for the mean and humble style — he weaponizes it. Watch the engine: the Synoptics' style is below human ambition, but their content is heavenly mysteries beyond the capacity of man (S3) — above human capacity. ★ Low vessel, high cargo. When the container cannot explain the contents, you must look above the container. That is EXACTLY yesterday's In what workshop could they have been so quickly fabricated? — the natural explanation is choked off on purpose, so that only the providential one is left standing. Enemy librarians, unschooled fishermen, a persecutor turned preacher: one engine, four exhibits.

And notice the stage change. Old Testament proofs are finished (§§3–10). Today the New Testament takes the stand — and Calvin builds a little courtroom procession: three humble evangelists → John the thunderbolt → Peter and Paul's letters → then the men themselves (S9–S11): a tax-collector, two boatmen, a persecutor. The section ends where Chapter 8 has been heading all along: the Holy Spirit must have been the teacher — ⚠️ and that modal will be on the exam.

The shape in one breath:

   S1        the fanfare  ── "how solid ARE the pillars!"
   S2–S5     writings I   ── Synoptics: humble style, heavenly content
   S6–S8     writings II  ── John's thunder; Peter & Paul's majesty
   S9–S10    the MEN      ── unschooled apostles; converted Paul
   S11       verdict      ── deny what you like — the circumstances
                             still prove a divine Teacher
   ⚠️ 시험 포인트: §11 의 논거는 문체 하나가 아니라 둘이다 —
      글(S1–S8)과 사람(S9–S11). 절반만 고르는 선지를 조심하라.

Today's 3 Big Points — write them down now:

  1. Exclamatory inversion: how solid ARE the pillars ...! (S1). The textbook exclamation keeps statement order — How solid the pillars are! Calvin flips it: how solid are the pillars. ⚠️ That V–S order is question syntax under an exclamation mark — a rhetorical exclamation on the border of a question (I promised you this line yesterday; here it is). ★ Rhetorical-elevated register uses inversion for force; don't "correct" it, and don't parse are as starting a real question — the ! settles the speech-act.
  2. The return of , is, that ... — and this time the subject stacks three men deep (S9). Day 46 S1 gave you An objection ..., is, ... fitted. Today: But one circumstance, (sufficient of itself to exalt their doctrine above the world), is, that Matthew, (who ...), Peter and John, (who ...), being all rude and illiterate, **had never learned** ... ⚠️ Two verbs to find: the copula is (locked in commas, singular — agreeing with one circumstance, not with three apostles) and the buried main verb of the that-clause, had never learned — five commas deep. ⚠️ being all rude and illiterate is a participle, NOT the verb. Cover the appositives with your hand and the skeleton appears.
  3. Two faces of Let — plus the FOURTH value of modal + have + p.p. ★ S7: let them read the Gospel of John, and ... they will find = challenge-let: imperative + and = a conditional (if they read, they will find). ★ S11: Let those dogs deny ..., still the very circumstances proclaim = concessive-let: imperative + still = even if they deny ..., still ... — no one is inviting the dogs to do anything. ⚠️ Same Let, opposite value; the tail decides (and → condition, still → concession). And inside S11: must have been the teacher = deductive certainty about the past (the evidence forces the conclusion that he was). Yesterday's museum had three values of modal + have + p.p.; ⚠️ this is the fourth — and it is NOT Day 46 S13's counterfactual must have utterly perished. There an if-clause made it unreal; here an evidence-clause (the circumstances proclaim) makes it an inference about what really happened. The frame decides everything. Again.

Eleven sentences. S6, S7, S9 and S11 will take real time. Let's go.

1 — Full Text (the whole § — 11 sentences, verbatim) –

11. When we proceed to the New Testament, how solid are the pillars by which its truth is supported! Three evangelists give a narrative in a mean and humble style. The proud often eye this simplicity with disdain, because they attend not to the principal heads of doctrine; for from these they might easily infer that these evangelists treat of heavenly mysteries beyond the capacity of man. Those who have the least particle of candour must be ashamed of their fastidiousness when they read the first chapter of Luke. Even our Saviour’s discourses, of which a summary is given by these three evangelists, ought to prevent every one from treating their writings with contempt. John, again, fulminating in majesty, strikes down more powerfully than any thunderbolt the petulance of those who refuse to submit to the obedience of faith. Let all those acute censors, whose highest pleasure it is to banish a reverential regard of Scripture from their own and other men’s hearts, come forward; let them read the Gospel of John, and, willing or unwilling, they will find a thousand sentences which will at least arouse them from their sloth; nay, which will burn into their consciences as with a hot iron, and check their derision. The same thing may be said of Peter and Paul, whose writings, though the greater part read them blindfold, exhibit a heavenly majesty, which in a manner binds and rivets every reader. But one circumstance, sufficient of itself to exalt their doctrine above the world, is, that Matthew, who was formerly fixed down to his money-table, Peter and John, who were employed with their little boats, being all rude and illiterate, had never learned in any human school that which they delivered to others. Paul, more over, who had not only been an avowed but a cruel and bloody foe, being changed into a new man, shows, by the sudden and unhoped-for change, that a heavenly power had compelled him to preach the doctrine which once he destroyed. Let those dogs deny that the Holy Spirit descended upon the apostles, or, if not, let them refuse credit to the history, still the very circumstances proclaim that the Holy Spirit must have been the teacher of those who, formerly contemptible among the people, all of a sudden began to discourse so magnificently of heavenly mysteries. ✅ Text note. All seven in Period 5. ℹ️ On the count. Eleven sentences, one paragraph. ① 최다 출제 예상: 문체 논증의 방향. ⚠️ 선지에 이런 게 나온다: "Calvin apologizes for the evangelists' crude style, urging readers to overlook it for the sake of the message." ✗ 정확히 거꾸로다. 칼빈은 문체의 낮음을 변명하지 않는다 — 낮은 그릇에 담긴 높은 내용물(S3 heavenly mysteries beyond the capacity of man) 자체가 증거다. 자연적 설명(수사학 훈련·인간 학교)이 막히니 섭리적 설명만 남는다. ★ 어제의 In what workshop…? 과 같은 엔진.

② 두 번째 함정: S7 의 willing or unwilling. ⚠️ "John's Gospel persuades only those already willing to submit" ✗ — 본문이 명시적으로 반대를 말한다: willing or unwilling, they will find a thousand sentences. 요한의 위엄은 독자의 동의를 기다리지 않는다. (단, 이것이 구원을 준다는 말은 아니다 — 한계선은 Period 6.)

③ 세 번째 함정: S8 의 read them blindfold. ⚠️ "Peter and Paul wrote blindly" ✗ — 눈가리개를 한 것은 독자 대다수(the greater part)이고, them 은 그들의 글이다. 대충 읽는 대다수에게도 하늘의 위엄이 binds and rivets 한다 — 삽입된 양보절(though …)이 주어와 동사를 갈라놓았을 뿐이다.

④ 네 번째 함정: S11 을 양보가 아니라 시인으로 읽기. ⚠️ "Calvin concedes that the descent of the Spirit cannot be proven from history" ✗ — Let those dogs deny …, still … 는 양보 명령문이다: 부인하려면 해 보라, 그래도(still) 정황 자체가 증명한다. 칼빈은 아무것도 내주지 않았다 — 상대가 다 부인해도 결론이 선다는 최강수다. ★ must have been = 반사실이 아니라 추론적 확실성 (Big Point 3).

S4★candour vs fastidiousness — a duel of trap words›

"Those who have the least particle of candour must be ashamed of their fastidiousness when they read the first chapter of Luke."

🔤 Morphology · 어형
  • candour — candidus(희다) → candid, candidate(흰 토가) — 마음이 흰 것 = 공정. '직설' 아님. KR: 공정성
  • fastidiousness — fastidium(구역질, 오만) — '꼼꼼함' 아님, 코를 쥐는 태도. KR: 결벽적 경멸
전체 해설 더보기

One point: candour = fairness of mind (not bluntness), and fastidiousness = sneering squeamishness (not attention to detail) — even one grain (the least particle) of the first shames the second, and the exhibit is Luke 1 (Period 7 tells you why that chapter, of all chapters).

4 — Today's Grammar Formulas –

Formula 1 — Exclamatory inversion (rhetorical register)

   How + ADJ + V + S (...)!
   평서 감탄문: How solid the pillars are!   (S-V 어순)
   수사 감탄문: How solid ARE the pillars!   (V-S 어순 — 의문 구문 + !)
   ⚠️ 함정 1: V-S 어순만 보고 "의문문인데 물음표가 틀렸다"로
      고치면 오답 — !가 화행(speech-act)을 결정한다.
   ⚠️ 함정 2: 일반 영작에서 남용 금지 — 격조 높은 수사문 전용.

Drill: 「신약의 증인들은 얼마나 견고한가!」 → When we examine these witnesses, how firm is the foundation on which our faith rests!

Formula 2 — Heavy subject + , is, that ... (콤마에 갇힌 copula + that절 보어)

   S(head) + (수식더미) , is , that + [주어스택 + (삽입들) + 본동사]
   ① copula 의 수일치는 주어 머리(head)를 따른다:
      one circumstance ... IS   (사도가 셋이어도 단수)
   ② that절 안에서도 같은 훈련: 삽입(관계절·분사구)을 괄호로
      걷어내고 본동사를 찾는다 (being ≠ 본동사).
   ⚠️ 함정: `, is,` 의 콤마는 19세기 관행이다. 오류로 고치지
      말고, 콤마 안의 is 를 정동사로 '수배'하라.

Drill: 「한 가지 사정 — 그 자체로 충분한 — 은, 어부들이 배운 적 없는 것을 전달했다는 것이다.」 → But one circumstance, sufficient of itself to silence the scoffers, is, that these fishermen, being wholly untrained, delivered to the world that which they had never learned.

Formula 3 — Let 의 두 얼굴 + 추론의 must have + p.p.

   ① 도전-Let (조건):  Let X V ..., and S will V
      = If X V-es, S will V          (S7: let them read, and
                                       they will find)
   ② 양보-Let (최강수): Let X V ..., still S + V
      = Even if X V-es, still S V-es (S11: Let them deny, still
                                       the circumstances proclaim)
   ⚠️ 꼬리로 판별: and → 조건 / still·yet → 양보.
   ③ must have + p.p. (증거절과 함께) = 과거 사실에 대한 추론적
      확신: "~였음에 틀림없다"
      ⚠️ Day 46 S13 의 반사실(must have utterly perished)과
        골격이 같다. if절(반사실) vs 증거절(추론) — 프레임이
        값을 정한다.

Drill: 「그들이 그 기록을 부인하게 두라 — 그래도 그 갑작스러운 변화 자체가, 하늘의 능력이 그를 가르쳤음에 틀림없다고 선포한다.」 → Let them deny the record if they will, still the sudden change itself proclaims that a heavenly power must have been his teacher.

6 — Background in 5 Minutes –

Where we are in the chapter's machine. Chapter 8 is the catalogue of rational confirmations — and §§1–2 opened it with Scripture's majesty vs human eloquence. Today's section is that theme's boldest variation: the majesty argument runs THROUGH the anti-eloquence. The Synoptics have no Ciceronian period, no Demosthenic thunder — and precisely there, content beyond the capacity of man rides in a cart. The argument is a fork: if Scripture is eloquent (John fulminating, the epistles' heavenly majesty), majesty testifies; if Scripture is plain (the Synoptics), the surplus of content over style testifies. Heads, God wins; tails, the sneerers lose.

The lineage — this argument has scars. The sneer is ancient: Celsus (2nd c.) mocked Christianity's founders as sailors and tax-gatherers; Origen flipped the taunt — unlettered men converting the world proves a power not in persuasive words of wisdom (1 Cor 2:4–5 was the engine long before Calvin). Augustine confessed the sneer from inside: young rhetor Augustine found Scripture unworthy next to Cicero (Conf. 3.5), and only later learned to read the lowliness as accommodation — God stooping to lift (Conf. 6.5). Calvin — a trained humanist who cut his teeth on a Seneca commentary (1532) and could weigh Latin prose with the best of his century — is not making a virtue of ignorance. A stylist certifies the anomaly: he knows exactly what human skill can produce, which is how he knows this surplus is not human skill. Centuries later Erich Auerbach gives the phenomenon its classic name — sermo humilis, the low style bearing the highest things — and apologetics from Paley to Warfield will keep re-filing S9–S10 (unschooled witnesses, the hostile witness converted) under "evidences."

The over-reading guardrail. ⚠️ Do not promote Calvin into an evidentialist. These are secondary helps (adminicula): §13 will say in so many words that such proofs serve only after the Spirit's inner testimony has established certainty in the heart (and I.7.4–5 already said faith does not stand on argument). S7 promises the censors arousal and a branded conscience — not conversion. The thousand sentences of John can stop a mouth without opening a heart. Keep the two registers apart on the exam: what the circumstances PROVE (a divine teacher — inexcusability's cousin) vs what the Spirit GIVES (certainty of faith). ★ And one more historical note: the man writing the harmony of the Evangelists into his argument will spend part of his life on it — Calvin's own Harmony of the Gospels (1555) is this section grown up.

7 — Scripture Connections –

  1. Rom 1:5 — ὑπακοὴ πίστεως (the obedience of faith) — S6. A verbatim Pauline phrase dropped without citation: the refusers are measured in the apostle's own coinage. Calvin's habit: the argument FOR Scripture is conducted IN Scripture's words.
  2. Luke 1:1–4 — S4's exhibit. Why "the first chapter of Luke," of all chapters? Its four-verse preface is the most polished periodic Greek in the NT — then the register drops into Hebraic narrative. One chapter shows the evangelist COULD write high style and chose the low: the simplicity is election, not incapacity. Candour's least particle must concede it.
  3. 1 Tim 4:2 — κεκαυστηριασμένων τὴν συνείδησιν — S7's burn into their consciences as with a hot iron. ★ A reversal: in Paul, the seared conscience is branded NUMB; in Calvin, John's sentences brand the conscience AWAKE. Same iron, opposite therapy — deliberate, and exam-worthy.
  4. Acts 4:13 — ἀγράμματοί εἰσιν καὶ ἰδιῶται — S9's rude and illiterate. Nearly a translation: the Sanhedrin's own astonished verdict on Peter and John becomes Calvin's premise. The hostile court supplies the adjective — yesterday's "hostile witness" method, again.
  5. Matt 9:9 (the τελώνιον) and Luke 5:1–11 — S9's money-table and little boats. The callings, compressed to two props. The diminutive little boats is doing argumentative work: small boats, small men, small school.
  6. Gal 1:13, 23 — preach the faith which once he destroyed (ἐπόρθει) — S10's preach the doctrine which once he destroyed. A near-quotation: the Judean churches' one-line summary of Paul's reversal is Calvin's entire S10 in miniature. What the churches glorified God for, Calvin enters as evidence.
  7. Acts 2 — S11's the history. The specific record the dogs may refuse credit to: the descent (2:1–4) AND the crowd's own inference — are not all these which speak Galileans? (2:7). The very circumstances argument is already inside the narrative being denied.
  8. Phil 3:2 — Βλέπετε τοὺς κύνας (Beware of dogs) — S11's those dogs. The epithet is borrowed polemic, not lost temper: Paul's word for those who gnaw at the gospel's credentials. Calvin cites by snarl.

8 — Exam Problems –

① 어법 — 밑줄 친 부분 중 틀린 것은?

How solid ①are the pillars by which this doctrine is supported! One circumstance, sufficient of itself to silence the scoffers, ②are, that fishermen, being all rude and illiterate, delivered to the world that which they had never learned. Let them deny the history, ③still the very facts proclaim that a heavenly power ④must have been their teacher.

✨ 답안 보기 (클릭)정답: ②. 주어 머리는 One circumstance(단수) — 수식더미(sufficient of itself ...)와 that절 안의 복수 명사(fishermen)에 홀리면 진다. is 로 고쳐야 한다 (Formula 2 ①). 출제 의도: ①은 Formula 1 의 수사 감탄 도치(정상), ③은 양보-Let 의 꼬리(정상), ④는 증거절과 함께 선 추론의 must have p.p.(정상) — 오늘의 세 공식을 정상 선지로 깔고, 어제부터 이틀째 조련한 무거운 주어 수일치를 함정으로 세웠다.

② 내용일치 — §11 의 논지와 일치하는 것은?

(a) Calvin apologizes for the Synoptics' crude style, asking readers to excuse it for the sake of their message. (b) Calvin argues that John's Gospel convinces only those who are already willing to submit to it. (c) Calvin holds that the apostles' lack of human schooling, set against what they actually delivered, points to the Holy Spirit as their teacher. (d) Calvin concedes that if opponents deny the descent of the Spirit, the argument from the apostles cannot proceed.

✨ 답안 보기 (클릭)정답: (c). S9 + S11 그대로: 배운 적 없는 자들의 출력 초과분이 하늘의 교사를 증명한다. 함정 해설: (a) 방향 뒤집기 — 문체의 낮음은 변명거리가 아니라 증거다(Examiner's Eye ①). (b) 본문 명시 반대 — willing or unwilling(S7, Examiner's Eye ②). (d) 방향 뒤집기 — S11 의 양보-Let 은 부인을 허용해도 결론이 선다는 최강수지, 논증의 중단이 아니다(Examiner's Eye ④).

③ 영작 — 오늘의 공식을 적용해 옮기시오

문제: 「이 증인들은 얼마나 견고한가! 그들로 하여금 그 기록을 불신하게 두라 — 그래도 그 갑작스러운 변화는, 하늘의 능력이 그들의 교사였음에 틀림없다고 선포한다. 그리고 한 가지 사정 — 그 자체로 충분한 — 은, 그들이 어떤 인간 학교에서도 자기들이 전달한 것을 배운 적이 없다는 것이다.」

(조건: Formula 1(How + adj + V + S!), Formula 3 ②(Let ..., still ...)와 ③(must have been), Formula 2(, is, that + 수일치)를 각각 사용할 것.)

모범 답안:

How solid are these witnesses! Let them refuse credit to the history, still the sudden change proclaims that a heavenly power must have been their teacher. And one circumstance, sufficient of itself, is, that they had never learned in any human school that which they delivered.

출제 의도 해설. 네 공식이 연속 산문에서 값을 유지하는지 본다. 자주 나오는 실패 넷:

  1. How solid these witnesses are! — 문법은 맞지만 ⚠️ 오늘의 과제가 아니다. 수사 감탄의 V-S 도치(How solid are these witnesses!)가 과녁.
  2. Let them refuse ..., and still ... — ✗ ⚠️ and 를 넣는 순간 도전-Let(조건)의 꼬리와 섞인다. 양보-Let 은 콤마 + still 로 바로 잇는다.
  3. must be their teacher — ✗ ⚠️ 과거 사실에 대한 추론은 must have been. 현재형이면 시제가 증거보다 앞서 나간다.
  4. one circumstance ... are, that ... — ✗ ⚠️ ①번 문제를 그대로 다시 틀리는 학생이 반드시 있다. 주어 머리 단수 → is. 그리고 , is, 의 콤마를 지우지 말 것 — 오늘은 이 19세기 골격 자체가 과녁이다.

9 — One-Line Wrap-up + Homework –

One-line summary. ⚠️ §11 은 신약 증언의 개시이고, 논거는 둘이다 — 글과 사람. 무대가 열리고(how solid are the pillars ...! — S1, 수사 감탄 도치), 공관복음의 mean and humble style(S2)이 먼저 선다: 교만한 자들은 문체를 비웃지만 그것은 the principal heads of doctrine 을 안 읽었기 때문이고(S3 — ⚠️ from these = 교리의 머리들), 조금의 공정성(the least particle of candour)만 있어도 누가복음 1장 앞에서 부끄러워진다(S4). 요약본만으로도 경멸은 금지되고(S5), 요한은 fulminating in majesty 벼락보다 강하게 내리친다(S6 — ⚠️ 목적어 the petulance 는 비교 부사구 뒤로 늦게 온다). 감찰자들은 나와서 읽어 보라 — willing or unwilling, 천 개의 문장이 잠을 깨우고 낙인처럼 태운다(S7 — 도전-Let). 베드로·바울의 글도 대충 읽는 대다수(the greater part read them blindfold)를 binds and rivets 한다(S8). 그리고 사람들: one circumstance, ..., is, that(S9 — ⚠️ 어제의 , is, 귀환, 수일치는 머리 단수) 세리와 어부들이 being all rude and illiterate — 분사지 동사가 아니다 — had never learned in any human school that which they delivered: 출력이 입력을 초과하면 초과분이 증거다. 바울의 sudden and unhoped-for change(S10 — 갈 1:23 근인용)가 네 번째 전시물이고, 평결: Let those dogs deny ..., still the very circumstances proclaim that the Holy Spirit must have been the teacher(S11 — ★ 양보-Let + 추론의 must have been). 낮은 그릇, 높은 내용물 — 자연적 설명이 질식하는 자리에서 섭리적 설명이 선다: 어제의 공방(workshop) 질문과 한 엔진이다.

And the three grammar points, restated:

  1. 수사 감탄 도치. ★ How + adj + V + S! — 의문 구문에 감탄 부호: 격조 높은 수사문의 힘 주기(S1). ⚠️ 평서 감탄(How solid the pillars are!)으로 "교정"하지 말 것 — !가 화행을 정한다.
  2. , is, that + 주어 스택. ★ copula 는 주어 머리를 따른다(one circumstance ... is); that절 안에서는 삽입(관계절 둘 + being 분사)을 괄호로 걷어내고 본동사 had never learned 를 수배한다(S9). ⚠️ being 을 동사로 앉히는 순간 전멸.
  3. Let 의 두 얼굴 + 네 번째 modal perfect. ★ 꼬리가 값을 정한다: ..., and S will V = 조건(도전, S7) / ..., still S V = 양보(최강수, S11). 그리고 must have been + 증거절 = 과거 사실에 대한 추론적 확신 — ⚠️ Day 46 S13 의 반사실(must have utterly perished)과 골격은 같고 값은 반대. 이틀, 네 값, 한 교훈: 프레임이 결정한다.

① 구조 복원. S9 를 네 개의 독립된 평서문으로 해체하라: (a) 뼈대 문장(One circumstance is that they had never learned what they delivered. 수준까지 압축), (b) Matthew 의 관계절을 독립시킨 문장, (c) Peter and John 의 관계절을 독립시킨 문장, (d) being all rude and illiterate 분사를 정동사(이유절)로 세운 문장. 그런 다음 한 줄로 답하라: (d)의 '이유'는 논증에서 왜 필수인가 — 만약 그들이 배운 자들이었다면 S9 의 증거력은 어떻게 되는가?

② 영작. S6 의 공식(타동사 + 비교 부사구 + 늦게 오는 목적어)과 S11 의 양보-Let 을 한 단락에 넣어, "비판자가 부인해도 정황 자체가 증언한다" 는 오늘의 최강수 논법을 다른 소재(예: 순교자들의 담대함, 교회의 존속)로 두세 문장 만들어라. 조건: must have + p.p. 를 추론(반사실 아님) 값으로 쓸 것. 그런 다음 한 줄로 답하라: 당신의 문단에서 '증거절' 역할을 하는 절은 무엇인가?

③ 내일 예습 한 줄. §12 는 이렇게 열린다: Add, moreover, that, for the best of reasons, the consent of the Church is not without its weight. — ★ 명령문 Add that ... 이 논거를 쌓는 장부 화법이고, not without its weight 는 이중부정 litotes(= 상당한 무게가 있다): 두 장치 모두 내일의 과녁이다. 무대도 바뀐다: 글(§11)에서 교회의 역사(§12)로 — 수많은 세대의 한결같은 순종, 진리 자체의 불굴의 힘. ⚠️ 준비해 둘 질문 하나: 칼빈은 I.7 에서 "교회가 성경을 승인해서 권위가 선다"는 로마의 논리를 격파했다. 그런데 §12 는 교회의 consent 를 증거로 세운다 — 모순인가? 힌트: 권위의 근거(foundation)와 신빙성의 방증(help)은 법정에서 다른 자리에 앉는다.
