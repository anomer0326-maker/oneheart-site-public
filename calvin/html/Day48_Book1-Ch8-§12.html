Day 48 — Book 1, Chapter 8, §12

0 — Orientation (1 minute) –

I left you a landmine question overnight, and today you get to watch Calvin walk straight across the minefield without losing a leg:

Calvin demolished Rome's logic in I.7 — "the Church's approval is what gives Scripture its authority." Yet §12 calls the Church's consent as evidence. Contradiction?

Answer it before Calvin does. The foundation and the help sit in different chairs in the courtroom. In I.7 Rome tried to seat the Church on the judge's bench — authorizing Scripture from above. Calvin threw her off it. In §12 he seats her in the witness stand — testifying from below. A witness does not create the truth of the case; a witness confirms it. Watch the very first sentence do this with surgical precision: the consent of the Church is not without its weight — weight, evidential weight. Not foundation. Not authority. Weight on a scale that was already tipped by the Spirit (I.7.4–5). If you catch that one word, you have caught the whole section — and defused the exam's favorite trap.

And notice what the argument itself looks like today. §11 heard the writings and the writers. §12 opens the history books: fifteen centuries of uniform obedience, waves of attackers — sophists' pens, emperors' swords — and Scripture flourished like the palm tree and continued invincible. Then the geography: not one city, not one nation, but a holy league of peoples with nothing else in common. Calvin is running Gamaliel's test at full scale (Acts 5:38–39): if this work were of men, it would have come to nought. It didn't. Draw the conclusion.

The shape in one breath:

   S1        the ledger entry ── "Add that the Church's consent has weight"
   S2        exhibit A        ── ages of obedience + survival vs Satan
   S3–S4     the siege        ── sophists' pens (S3), princes' swords (S4)
   S5–S6     the inference    ── could it have resisted by human aid? →
                                 divine origin, its own energy
   S7–S8     exhibit B        ── not one city/nation: a worldwide holy league
   S9        exhibit C        ── agreement of the diverse + piety of the lights

Today's 3 Big Points — write them down now:

  1. The ledger imperative: Add, moreover, that ... (S1) and its clipped return Add that ... (S7). An imperative with no drama in it — Calvin is not commanding you to do anything; he is a bookkeeper telling you to enter the next item in the column of proofs. The whole that-clause is the object of Add. ⚠️ Don't read it as an emotional appeal; read it as accounting. And watch the second occurrence shed its robes: S1 arrives formal (, moreover, ... for the best of reasons,), S7 arrives bare (Add that) — the bookkeeper is moving faster because the column is filling up.
  2. The litotes battery — three double negatives in one section, all meaning MORE, not less. not without its weight (S1), not to be accounted of no consequence (S2), no small weight (S9). ★ Litotes = negating the negative to assert the positive with reserve — the rhetoric of a man understating what he could shout. ⚠️ The exam trap writes these as concessions ("Calvin admits the consent has only slight weight") — direction flip. Kill it on sight. not without weight = weighty; no small weight = great weight.
  3. The modal-perfect museum opens two new wings (S5, S9). Day 46 gave you the counterfactual statement (must have utterly perished), Day 47 the inference (must have been the teacher). Today: ★ S5 how could it have resisted if it had trusted only to human aid? = counterfactual INTERROGATIVE — a rhetorical question whose only answer is it could not have — negation delivered as a question. ★ S9 an agreement which a Divine Providence alone could have produced = alone could have p.p. — nothing else could have produced it; it exists; therefore Providence did — an inference wearing a counterfactual's clothes. ⚠️ Same verb cluster, four days, five values. The frame decides. Every time.

Nine sentences. S1, S2, S5 and S9 take the heavy time. Let's go.

1 — Full Text (the whole § — 9 sentences, verbatim) –

12. Add, moreover, that, for the best of reasons, the consent of the Church is not without its weight. For it is not to be accounted of no consequence, that, from the first publication of Scripture, so many ages have uniformly concurred in yielding obedience to it, and that, notwithstanding of the many extraordinary attempts which Satan and the whole world have made to oppress and overthrow it, or completely efface it from the memory of men, it has flourished like the palm tree and continued invincible. Though in old times there was scarcely a sophist or orator of any note who did not exert his powers against it, their efforts proved unavailing. The powers of the earth armed themselves for its destruction, but all their attempts vanished into smoke. When thus powerfully assailed on every side, how could it have resisted if it had trusted only to human aid? Nay, its divine origin is more completely established by the fact, that when all human wishes were against it, it advanced by its own energy. Add that it was not a single city or a single nation that concurred in receiving and embracing it. Its authority was recognised as far and as wide as the world extends—various nations who had nothing else in common entering for this purpose into a holy league. Moreover, while we ought to attach the greatest weight to the agreement of minds so diversified, and in all other things so much at variance with each other—an agreement which a Divine Providence alone could have produced—it adds no small weight to the whole when we attend to the piety of those who thus agree; not of all of them indeed, but of those in whom as lights God was pleased that his Church should shine. ✅ Text note. One paragraph only — no Beveridge break; the ledger runs unbroken from first entry to last. ⚠️ Now run the standing Day 42 checks. Every stranded comparative, every so/too/such without a partner, every pronoun without an antecedent. Correlatives: **as** far and **as** wide **as** the world extends (S8) ✓ — a doubled equative sharing one as-tail; nothing stranded. **so** many ages (S2) — ⚠️ no that-partner, and none needed: so many here is plain quantity ("such a great number"), not degree-with-result. **so** diversified ... **so** much at variance (S9) — same verdict: intensifiers without result clauses; the degree anchor is the standing miracle of their agreeing at all. **more** completely (S6) — ⚠️ mind the ellipsis: more completely than mere survival would establish it; the comparison reaches back to S2–S5's defensive argument. Flag, resolve, move on. Pronouns: it (S2, seven times by the end) = Scripture throughout — ⚠️ but the FIRST it (it is not to be accounted) is a dummy: extraposition subject for the twin that-clauses. Sort the dummies from the real ones or S2 dissolves. their (S3) = the sophists and orators — ⚠️ notional plural: the antecedent is scarcely a sophist or orator (singular in form), but sense demands plural; Beveridge rides the sense. their (S4) = the powers of the earth ✓. it adds (S9) — ⚠️ flagged: ambient/anticipatory it pointing forward to the when-clause (attending to their piety is what adds the weight), not Scripture this time. them / those (S9 tail) = the agreeing minds; of all of them ... of those both hang from the piety — supply it: the piety, not of all of them, but of those ... Trap words on patrol: consequence (S2) = importance — nothing follows from anything. publication (S2) = making public (first promulgation) — no printing press in Moses' day. concurred (S2, S7) = agreed together (con + currere, ran together) — no traffic accident. of any note (S3) = of any distinction — nobody is writing music. unavailing (S3) = useless, without effect (a + valere) — not "unavailable." Nay (S6) = escalation: "more than that" — not a refusal. energy (S6) = inherent operative power (Gk. energeia) — not enthusiasm. league (S8) = alliance, covenant (Lat. ligare, to bind) — not a sports division and not a unit of distance. All eight in Period 5. ℹ️ On the count. Nine sentences, one paragraph. The two giants are S2 (the twin-that exhibit, split by the page turn) and S9 (the double-dash appositive with the semicolon tail). The semicolon inside S9 does NOT end a sentence — the tail (not of all of them ...) is an elliptical afterthought still hanging from the piety. The count stands at 9.

Examiner's Eye — 네 개를 찍어 둔다.

① 최다 출제 예상: I.7 과의 관계. ⚠️ 선지에 이런 게 나온다: "Calvin here reverses his earlier position and grounds the authority of Scripture on the Church's approval." ✗ 정반대다. I.7 이 격파한 것은 교회를 재판관석(권위의 근거)에 앉히는 로마의 논리이고, §12 가 세우는 것은 교회를 증인석(신빙성의 방증)에 앉히는 것이다. 열쇠 단어는 S1 의 weight — 저울에 얹는 무게지, 건물을 받치는 기초가 아니다. §13 이 이를 명문화한다(secondary helps to our weakness).

② 두 번째 함정: litotes 를 양보로 읽기. ⚠️ "Calvin concedes that the Church's consent carries only slight weight" ✗ — not without its weight(S1)·not ... of no consequence(S2)·no small weight(S9)는 모두 절제된 강조다: 상당한 무게가 있다. 이중부정 = 약화가 아니라 격조 있는 확언(Big Point 2).

③ 세 번째 함정: S5 를 열린 질문으로 읽기. ⚠️ "Calvin leaves open the question whether Scripture survived by human aid" ✗ — how could it have resisted if it had trusted only to human aid? 는 반사실 수사의문이다: 유일한 답은 못 버텼다. S6 의 Nay 가 즉시 도장을 찍는다: 인간의 도움이 아니라 its own energy.

④ 네 번째 함정: S9 꼬리의 범위. ⚠️ "Calvin praises the piety of all who have ever agreed to Scripture" ✗ — 본문이 명시적으로 자른다: not of all of them indeed, but of those in whom as lights God was pleased that his Church should shine. 무게를 더하는 것은 전원의 경건이 아니라 등불들의 경건이다. 범위 초과 선지의 교과서적 표본.

S4★the swords attack — and vanish into smoke›

"The powers of the earth armed themselves for its destruction, but all their attempts vanished into smoke."

전체 해설 더보기

One point and we move: vanished into smoke = came to nothing — and the image is loaded twice over. The attackers lit real fires (Diocletian burned the books), and their attempts, not the books, became the smoke; behind it stands the Psalter's verdict on the wicked — into smoke shall they consume away (Ps 37:20) — and Psalm 2's kings of the earth setting themselves in vain. The pens failed (S3); now the swords fail (S4). Two-front siege, zero progress.

  • variance < Lat. variare (to change) → various, variegated; providence < pro + videre (to see ahead) → provision, prudent (!); piety < Lat. pietas — dutiful reverence (Day 4's word, returned as evidence).
💬 Idiom · 관용
  • attach weight to X = treat X as important; at variance with X = in conflict with X; be pleased that ... = sovereignly will that ... (of God); as lights = as beacons/luminaries.
쉬운 영어 / Modern English

Moreover, granted that the greatest weight belongs to this agreement of minds so diverse — and in everything else so much in conflict — an agreement only divine Providence could have brought about, it adds considerable weight when we consider the godliness of those who agree: not all of them, certainly, but those in whom God chose to make his Church shine as lights.

Key changes · 올·현대 표현
  • attach the greatest weight to → give the greatest weight to
  • at variance with each other → in conflict with each other
  • it adds no small weight → it adds considerable weight
  • God was pleased that his Church should shine → God chose to make his Church shine.

4 — Today's Grammar Formulas –

Formula 1 — the ledger imperative

  Add(, moreover,) (that) + S + V ...        ← 논거 적립 명령문
  = "다음 항목을 증거 장부에 기입하라"
  ⚠️ 감정적 명령이 아니라 회계 화법 — that절 전체가 Add 의 목적어
  ⚠️ 두 번째 등장부터는 장식이 벗겨진다: Add that ... (S7)

Transformation drill. 순교자 논증(§13 예고)을 장부에 기입하라: → Add, moreover, that a doctrine sealed with the blood of so many witnesses is not to be lightly dismissed. (S1 의 격식형 그대로 — , moreover, 삽입과 that절 목적어를 확인.)

Formula 2 — the litotes battery

  not without + N          = N 이 실제로 있다     (S1)
  not ... of no + N        = N 이 크다           (S2)
  no small + N             = 큰 N               (S9)
  ⚠️ 셋 다 절제된 강조(understatement) — "약간의/조금의"로
     읽는 순간 방향이 뒤집힌다. 이중부정 = 격조 있는 확언.

Transformation drill. "교부들의 증언은 상당한 권위를 갖는다"를 절제 화법으로: → The testimony of the fathers is not without its authority, and their agreement is of no small consequence. (litotes 두 종을 한 문장에 — 뜻은 강하고 목소리는 낮게.)

Formula 3 — the modal-perfect museum, wings five and six

  ① How could S have p.p. ..., if S had p.p. ...?   (S5)
     = 반사실 수사의문 — 유일한 답: "could not have"
  ② X alone could have p.p. + (그런데 결과가 실존)     (S9)
     = "X 외엔 불가능 → 결과가 있다 → X 가 했다" (추론)
  ⚠️ 골격은 같은 modal + have + p.p. — 값은 프레임이 정한다:
     if절 = 반사실 / 의문문 = 강한 부정 / alone + 실존 = 추론

Transformation drill. 교회의 존속으로: → How could the Church have survived so many centuries of persecution, if she had leaned only on the arm of princes? Such endurance the Spirit of God alone could have produced. (①과 ②를 연속 배치 — S5→S9 의 실제 순서 그대로.)

6 — Background in 5 Minutes –

The doctrine at stake: what a "help" may do that a "foundation" may not. Chapter 7 settled WHERE certainty comes from: the internal testimony of the Holy Spirit — not the Church's decree. Chapter 8 has been listing adminicula — secondary helps, props for our weakness — and §12 adds the boldest one: the Church herself, re-admitted through the servants' entrance. Underline the difference: Rome says the Church's consent makes Scripture authoritative; Calvin says the Church's consent confirms to us what was authoritative before she spoke. The witness stand, not the bench. §13 will bolt this down in so many words: these proofs are secondary helps to our weakness, useless to produce faith on their own. ⚠️ Over-reading guardrail: do not turn Calvin into an evidentialist. The survival argument persuades the pious and silences the slanderer; it converts nobody. If you leave today thinking Calvin proves inspiration from church history, you have put the cart before the horse he spent Chapter 7 harnessing.

The history behind the sentences. S3's sophists have names: Celsus (2nd c., The True Word, dismantled by Origen), Porphyry (3rd c., fifteen books Against the Christians), Lucian the satirist, the emperor Julian "the Apostate" with his Against the Galileans. Now the delicious irony Calvin's readers knew: Porphyry's attack survives only in fragments — the empire ordered it burned in 448 — while the text he attacked sits open on every lectern in Europe. The attack was effaced; the target proved invincible. S4's powers of the earth: above all Diocletian, whose edict of 303 ordered every copy of the Scriptures surrendered and burned — the one imperial persecution aimed at the book itself, efface it from the memory of men as literal state policy. Twenty-two years later Constantine was commissioning fifty Bibles for Constantinople at public expense. Smoke, indeed. And S8's holy league rings with Calvin's own decade — the Schmalkaldic League (1531) fresh in every reader's ear: princes bind themselves for interest; these nations, with nothing else in common, were bound by the Book alone.

Lineage and afterlife. The argument is patristic before it is Calvin's: Augustine (City of God XXII.5) marveled that the world believed fishermen against all its own wishes — S6's against all human wishes is nearly a citation. Forward: the endurance-and-spread argument became a fixture of classical apologetics (Grotius, Paley), and its abuse — church history as a demonstration compelling faith — is exactly what Calvin's weight-not-foundation distinction forbids. The moderns who remembered the distinction (Bavinck: the testimonium is the principium, external proofs its servants) are Calvin's true heirs here.

7 — Scripture Connections –

  1. Ps 92:12 — The righteous shall flourish like the palm tree — S2's flourished like the palm tree. Hebrew: צַדִּיק כַּתָּמָר יִפְרָח (tsaddiq katamar yifrach). Calvin lifts the psalm's emblem for the righteous man and pins it on the Book — Scripture personified as the righteous sufferer who thrives under oppression. The transfer is the exegesis: what God does for his saints, he does for his Word.
  2. Ps 2:1–4 (with Acts 4:25–26) — S4's The powers of the earth armed themselves. The kings of the earth set themselves ... against the Lord — and he that sitteth in the heavens shall laugh. The apostles already read Ps 2 as persecution commentary (Acts 4); Calvin extends the same reading to the war on Scripture.
  3. Ps 37:20 (cf. Ps 68:2) — S4's vanished into smoke. The enemies of the Lord ... into smoke shall they consume away. The arsonists become the smoke — the image executes the reversal.
  4. Isa 40:8 (with 1 Pet 1:24–25) — S2/S6's invincible endurance. The grass withereth ... but the word of our God shall stand for ever. Gk. (LXX/Peter): τὸ ῥῆμα κυρίου μένει εἰς τὸν αἰῶνα. §12 is this verse argued from the historical record.
  5. Acts 5:38–39 — the logical engine of S5–S6. Gamaliel: if this counsel or this work be of men, it will come to nought: but if it be of God, ye cannot overthrow it. Calvin runs the test across fifteen centuries and reports the result: not overthrown — therefore not of men.
  6. Isa 2:2–3 — S7–S8's holy league of the nations. All nations shall flow unto it ... and many people shall go and say, Come ye. The prophesied convergence of peoples upon the word of the LORD, historicized as the worldwide reception of Scripture.
  7. Phil 2:15 (cf. Matt 5:14) — S9's in whom as lights ... his Church should shine. Gk. φωστῆρες ἐν κόσμῳ (phōstēres en kosmō) — luminaries in the world. Calvin narrows the psalmic "all" to the Pauline "lights": the piety that weighs is the piety God kindled.

8 — Exam Problems –

① 어법 — 밑줄 친 부분 중 틀린 것은?

①Add, moreover, that the uniform consent of so many ages is ②not without its weight. When assailed on every side, how could Scripture ③have resisted if it ④trusted only to human aid?

✨ 답안 보기 (클릭)정답: ④. 반사실 조건절의 시제 — 과거 사실의 반대는 if it **had trusted** (had + p.p.). 주절의 could have resisted(③, 정상)와 짝을 이루는 과거완료가 와야 한다 (Formula 3 ①). 출제 의도: ①은 장부 명령문(Formula 1, 정상), ②는 litotes(Formula 2, 정상), ③은 반사실 수사의문의 modal perfect(정상) — 오늘의 세 공식을 정상 선지로 깔고, 반사실 조건절 시제 일치를 함정으로 세웠다. Day 46 부터 사흘째 조련한 그 골격이다.

② 내용일치 — §12 의 논지와 일치하는 것은?

(a) Calvin reverses his teaching in I.7 and grounds the authority of Scripture on the approval of the Church. (b) Calvin argues that Scripture's survival of every assault, and its reception by nations having nothing else in common, confirm its divine origin as secondary evidence. (c) Calvin concedes that the consent of the Church carries only slight weight compared with the internal testimony of the Spirit. (d) Calvin attributes the added weight of the argument to the piety of all who have ever professed obedience to Scripture.

✨ 답안 보기 (클릭)정답: (b). 시간(존속)·공간(만국 수용)의 두 증거가 방증(help)으로서 신적 기원을 확인한다 — S2–S8 그대로. 함정 해설: (a) 방향 뒤집기 — weight ≠ foundation; 교회는 증인석이지 재판관석이 아니다(Examiner's Eye ①). (c) litotes 를 양보로 오독 — not without its weight·no small weight 는 절제된 강조다(Examiner's Eye ②). (d) 범위 초과 — not of all of them indeed, but of those in whom as lights ...(S9 꼬리, Examiner's Eye ④).

③ 영작 — 오늘의 공식을 적용해 옮기시오

문제: 「더하여, 그토록 많은 세대의 한결같은 동의는 상당한 무게를 갖는다는 것을 적으라. 사방에서 공격받았을 때, 그것이 인간의 도움에만 의지했다면 어떻게 버틸 수 있었겠는가? 그러한 존속은 오직 하나님의 섭리만이 만들어 낼 수 있었다.」

(조건: Formula 1(Add that), Formula 2(litotes 1회 이상), Formula 3 ①(반사실 수사의문)과 ②(alone could have p.p.)를 각각 사용할 것.)

모범 답안:

Add, moreover, that the uniform consent of so many ages is not without its weight. When assailed on every side, how could it have resisted if it had trusted only to human aid? Such endurance a Divine Providence alone could have produced.

출제 의도 해설. 네 장치가 연속 산문에서 값을 유지하는지 본다. 자주 나오는 실패 넷:

  1. Add that the consent has great weight. — 뜻은 맞지만 ⚠️ litotes 조건 미충족. 오늘의 과녁은 절제 화법: is not without its weight.
  2. how could it resist if it had trusted ... — ✗ ⚠️ 주절의 modal perfect 탈락. 과거 반사실은 could have resisted. 현재형 resist 는 시제가 조건절과 어긋난다.
  3. only a Divine Providence could produce it — ✗ ⚠️ 과거의 실현된 결과에 대한 추론은 could have produced. 그리고 alone 의 자리: 명사 뒤(a Divine Providence alone)가 S9 의 원형이다.
  4. Add, that ... — ✗ ⚠️ Add 바로 뒤엔 콤마가 없다. S1 의 콤마들은 , moreover, 삽입의 것이다. 삽입 없이 쓰면 Add that ... (S7).

9 — One-Line Wrap-up + Homework –

One-line summary. ⚠️ §12 는 교회를 재판관석이 아니라 증인석에 앉힌다 — 열쇠 단어는 weight(방증의 무게)다. 장부가 열리고(Add, moreover, that ... — S1, 회계 화법의 명령문), 첫 기입: 교회의 consent 는 not without its weight(S1 — litotes 제1탄). 증거 A는 시간이다: 모든 세대의 한결같은 순종, 그리고 사탄과 온 세상의 말살 기도(efface it from the memory of men — 디오클레티아누스의 303년 칙령이 문자 그대로 이것이다)에도 flourished like the palm tree and continued invincible(S2 — 가주어 + 쌍둥이 that절, litotes 제2탄, 시 92:12). 펜이 덤볐고(scarcely a sophist ... who did not = 거의 전원이 덤볐다는 이중부정, S3), 칼이 덤볐으나 vanished into smoke(S4 — 시 2편·시 37:20). 그렇다면: how could it have resisted if it had trusted only to human aid?(S5 — ★ 반사실 수사의문, 답은 "못 버텼다") — Nay, 생존을 넘어 전진했다, by its own energy(S6 — 가말리엘의 검증, 행 5:38–39). 둘째 기입(Add that, S7 — 장식을 벗은 장부 화법 + 부정 it-cleft): 한 도시·한 민족이 아니라 as far and as wide as the world extends, 아무 공통점 없는 민족들의 holy league(S8 — 절대구문 + 16세기의 정치 언어). 관석: 그 다양한 정신들의 일치는 a Divine Providence alone could have produced(S9 — ★ alone could have p.p. = 추론), 거기에 no small weight(litotes 제3탄)를 더하는 것은 전원이 아니라 등불들의 경건이다(not of all of them ..., but of those in whom as lights God was pleased that his Church should shine — 빌 2:15). 기초는 성령의 내적 증언(I.7), 교회사는 저울 위의 무게 — 방증은 많고, 기초는 하나다.

And the three grammar points, restated:

  1. 장부 명령문. ★ Add(, moreover,) that + S V — that절 전체가 목적어인 적립 화법; 두 번째부터는 Add that 으로 옷을 벗는다(S1→S7). ⚠️ 감정적 명령으로 읽지 말 것 — 회계다.
  2. Litotes 3연타. ★ not without N / not ... of no N / no small N = 절제된 강조 — 셋 다 "크다"는 뜻이다(S1·S2·S9). ⚠️ "약간의"로 읽는 순간 방향이 뒤집힌다 — 양보 아님, 확언임.
  3. Modal perfect 제5·6관. ★ 반사실 수사의문 how could S have p.p. ..., if S had p.p.?(S5, 답은 강한 부정) / alone could have p.p. + 실존하는 결과 = 추론(S9). ⚠️ Day 46 반사실 → Day 47 추론 must → Day 48 의문형·alone형 — 골격은 하나, 값은 프레임이 정한다. 나흘째, 여섯 값, 한 교훈.

Homework (10 minutes).

① 구조 복원. S9 를 네 개의 독립된 평서문으로 해체하라: (a) while절을 독립시킨 문장(We ought to attach the greatest weight to the agreement of minds so diversified. 수준까지), (b) 대시 사이 동격 섬을 독립시킨 문장(alone could have produced 유지), (c) 주절을 독립시킨 문장(ambient it 을 해소해 Attending to their piety adds no small weight. 형태로), (d) 세미콜론 꼬리를 완전문으로 세운 문장(생략된 the piety 를 복원할 것). 그런 다음 한 줄로 답하라: (d)의 범위 제한("not of all of them")은 논증의 정직성에 왜 필수인가 — 만약 칼빈이 '동의한 전원의 경건'을 주장했다면 반대자는 어디를 찔렀겠는가?

② 영작. Formula 1(장부 명령문)과 Formula 3 ①(반사실 수사의문)을 한 단락에 넣어, "인간의 힘만으로는 설명되지 않는 존속" 논법을 다른 소재(예: 박해 아래의 교회, 이스라엘의 보존)로 두세 문장 만들어라. 조건: litotes 를 최소 1회 사용할 것. 그런 다음 한 줄로 답하라: 당신의 문단에서 수사의문의 '유일한 답'은 무엇이며, 독자가 그 답을 스스로 말하게 만드는 장치는 무엇인가?

③ 내일 예습 한 줄. §13 은 이렇게 열린다: Again, with what confidence does it become us to subscribe to a doctrine attested and confirmed by the blood of so many saints? — ★ with what confidence does it become us — 의문 부사구 전치 + 도치 + 비인칭 it becomes us(~하는 것이 우리에게 합당하다): 첫 문장부터 삼중 장치다. 무대는 순교자들: 피로 봉인된 증언(sealed with the blood of so many witnesses), 단 광신이 아니라 sober godly zeal 로. 그리고 장이 닫히며 8장 전체의 자물쇠가 채워진다: 이 모든 증거는 secondary helps to our weakness — 성령의 내적 증언 없이는 신앙을 낳지 못한다. ⚠️ 준비해 둘 질문 하나: §12 까지 쌓은 증거의 장부를 §13 이 스스로 "이것으로는 믿음이 서지 않는다"고 격하한다 — 그렇다면 칼빈은 왜 열세 절이나 증거를 쌓았는가? 힌트: 증거의 수신인이 둘이다 — 이미 믿는 자의 확증(confirm)과 비방자의 입막음(vindicate). 어느 쪽도 '창조'가 아니다.
